DICOM Studio Support

Get help with local imports, viewing tools, subscriptions, and purchase restoration.

Protect medical information. Never attach or paste DICOM files, medical images, patient names, identifiers, file paths, or health information into a public support request.

Before requesting help

  1. Confirm the selected folder or ZIP contains DICOM files.
  2. Keep the app open while a large study imports.
  3. For purchase access, open Settings and choose Restore Purchases.
  4. Confirm the iPhone is using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Contact support

Open a support request using the button below. Include the app version, iOS version, iPhone model, and a general description of the problem without any medical or identifying content.

Open a support request

Subscription management

Apple processes payments and refunds. You can view, change, or cancel a subscription in Apple subscription settings. For billing or refund requests, use Report a Problem.

Medical-use limitation

DICOM Studio is for education, organization, and non-diagnostic image review. It is not emergency support, medical advice, or a diagnostic service.
